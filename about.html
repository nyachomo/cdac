Our Story

School History

Sunrise Academy was founded in 1987 by a group of visionary educators who believed that every child deserved access to a world-class education rooted in values, discipline, and innovation. What began as a modest institution of 120 students and 12 teachers has grown into one of the region's most respected centres of learning.

Through the decades, the Academy has navigated significant milestones — the expansion of our secondary campus in 1999, the introduction of our science and technology wing in 2007, and the launch of our digital learning programme in 2018. Each era has brought new growth while preserving the founding values that define us.

Today, Sunrise Academy is home to over 2,400 students, 160 dedicated staff members, and a vibrant alumni network spanning five continents. Our graduates are doctors, engineers, artists, entrepreneurs, and public servants — a testament to the Academy's enduring impact.

What Drives Us

Vision & Mission

🔭

Our Vision

To be a leading institution that inspires learners to achieve their fullest potential — academically, morally, and socially — and to contribute meaningfully to a rapidly changing global community.

🎯

Our Mission

To provide a nurturing, inclusive, and intellectually stimulating environment where every student is equipped with the knowledge, skills, and values necessary to lead, innovate, and serve with integrity.

What We Stand For

Core Values

🌟 Excellence

We hold ourselves to the highest academic and personal standards, encouraging every learner to go beyond the ordinary and strive for distinction in all they do.

🤝 Integrity

Honesty, transparency, and ethical conduct are the cornerstones of life at Sunrise Academy — in the classroom, on the field, and in our community interactions.

💡 Innovation

We cultivate curious, creative minds that are not afraid to question, experiment, and reimagine solutions to the challenges facing our world today and tomorrow.

🌍 Inclusivity

Every student, regardless of background or ability, belongs here. We celebrate diversity and ensure that our school community reflects the richness of our society.

🌱 Responsibility

We nurture a deep sense of personal, social, and environmental responsibility — preparing students to be thoughtful stewards of their communities and the planet.

❤️ Compassion

We believe in the power of empathy and service. Our students are taught to look outward, lend a hand, and lead with kindness in everything they do.
