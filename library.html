📋 Borrowing Rules

1
Students may borrow up to 3 books at a time using their student ID card.
2
Books must be returned within 14 days. Renewals can be done once at the library desk.
3
A fine of Ksh 5 per day is charged for overdue books.
4
Lost or damaged books must be replaced or paid for at the current market price.
5
Reference books, newspapers, and journals are not available for borrowing — in-library use only.
6
Food and drinks are strictly prohibited inside the library at all times.
7
Mobile phones must be silenced and phone calls are not permitted inside the library.
