Mental Health Resources

We take mental health as seriously as physical health. Every student has access to confidential support services.

🧠
Support

School Counsellor

Our resident counsellor offers one-on-one confidential sessions for stress, anxiety, grief, peer pressure, and personal challenges. Book through the clinic or guidance office.

🤝
Peer Support

Wellness Ambassadors

Trained student wellness ambassadors are available during break and lunch periods. They provide a friendly ear and can guide you to professional help when needed.

📞
Helpline

Mental Health Helpline

Students in crisis can call the national youth helpline 1199 (free, 24/7). The school also operates an anonymous text support line: SMS "HELP" to 22100.

Health Tips for Students

Small daily habits make a big difference to your energy, focus, and overall wellbeing.

💧
Stay Hydrated
Drink at least 8 glasses of water per day. Dehydration reduces concentration.
🥦
Eat Balanced Meals
Include vegetables, proteins, and whole grains in every meal from the canteen.
😴
Get Enough Sleep
Aim for 8–10 hours each night. Sleep boosts memory and academic performance.
🏃
Stay Active
At least 30 minutes of physical activity daily — use the sports grounds!
🧼
Practise Good Hygiene
Wash hands regularly, especially before meals and after using washrooms.
🚭
Avoid Harmful Substances
The school is a drug, alcohol, and tobacco-free zone. Seek help if you need it.
👓
Protect Your Eyes
Take screen breaks every 20 minutes. Visit the clinic if you experience eye strain.
💬
Talk About It
Don't bottle up stress. Talk to a friend, teacher, nurse, or counsellor.
